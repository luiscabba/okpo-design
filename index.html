Calm by default. Detail one level down.

How OkPo’s brand console, earner app and earner page are built. Five shapes carry five meanings and everything else is ink, so the moments that pay an earner can still take over the screen. Every specimen below is the real component, styled from tokens.css.

Nine principles

They came from the Playbook redesign and now apply to every view.

1Start with something they recognise

The Playbook opens on the book from onboarding.

2One job per screen

Today is the mission. Wallet is the money.

3Say it once

Cards don’t repeat what the contents already say.

4Detail one level at a time

Book, part, line, source. IDs and trust on tap.

5Quiet until something needs you

“1 to check” only when due. No always-on strip.

6Few named groups, each with a shape

Facts, Voice, Rules. Shape first, then word.

7Plain words

“Today’s mission”, not “brief v7”.

8Fits one screen at rest

If the first view scrolls, it’s doing two jobs.

9Show it working

“See it in a post” beats a paragraph about a rule.

Five shapes, five meanings

Colour and shape are reserved for the five things people need to tell apart at a glance. Everything else is ink, so a shape never means two things. On light a glaze is graphic only; coloured text uses its label shade.

Tone-on-tone backdrop, clear glass

Three tiles a shade off the ground sit behind every screen: quarter top left, petals right, half rising from the bottom. Crisp, never blurred, never a glaze colour. Content sits on clear glass; anything that floats is strong glass. Today fits the phone at rest.

Strong glass swaps the gradient for --glass-strong: tab bar, sheets, banners, toasts.

Liquid glass, the book only

.liquid and .liquid.cover: blur 22px, saturate 1.75, a sheen overlay, rim highlights, and crisp Facts, Voice and Rules tiles behind so the glass has something to bend. Used on the Playbook book and the “Your playbook is live” moment, nowhere else.

For AI coders

Point your coding agent at DESIGN.md and import tokens.css. The short list it must never break:

  • Five shapes, five meanings. Everything else is ink.
  • No icons in the console nav.
  • Rule IDs, trust and dates one tap down, never upfront.
  • Campaign rules live in the campaign, never in the Playbook.
  • “Today’s mission” for earners; “brief” is brand-side only.
  • No sloppy edges on product controls. No glaze colour behind a screen.
  • Never show the earner their quality number.
  • No full-screen moments in the brand console.
  • No blur on backdrop tiles. No em dashes in copy.
