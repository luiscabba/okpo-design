Tone-on-tone backdrop, clear glass

Three tiles a shade off the ground sit behind every screen: quarter top left, petals right, half rising from the bottom. Crisp, never blurred, never a glaze colour. Content sits on clear glass; anything that floats is strong glass.

Rules, facts and talking points

Rules say what a post must never do, and each has an ID. Facts are the only claims the AI may make. Talking points say what a post leads with; they live in each campaign’s brief, steer the mix, and never override a rule.

For AI coders

Point your coding agent at DESIGN.md and import tokens.css. The short list it must never break:

  • No sloppy edges on product controls.
  • No glaze colour behind a screen.
  • Never show the earner their quality number.
  • Say “talking point”, never “angle”.
  • No full-screen moments in the brand console.
  • No blur on backdrop tiles. No em dashes in copy.
